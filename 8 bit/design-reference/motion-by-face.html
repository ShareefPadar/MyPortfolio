06 — WHY LONG HEADINGS CAN'T TYPEthe carve-out
The same case study H1, typed instead of scanned. 72 characters at 45ms — over three seconds before the visitor can read your title.

07 — RULESfinal
  • Typewriter runs at 45ms per character, never slower
  • Nothing types longer than 1.5s total — if it would, it scans instead
  • Caret is a blue block, blinking at steps(1), removed when typing ends
  • Scanline applies to a container as a whole, never to its children
  • Body and mono stay binary — no scanline over text being read
  • Everything fires once on scroll-in, never on scroll-back
Never
  • Type a sentence-length heading or any body copy
  • Scanline a paragraph — banding over reading material
  • Type more than one element at a time on screen
  • Leave the caret blinking after the line finishes
