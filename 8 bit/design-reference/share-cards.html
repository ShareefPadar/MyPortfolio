Generating them
You're on Next.js and Vercel, so these don't need to be exported by hand — next/og renders JSX to an image at request time. The card markup above is close to what you'd paste in, and every case study gets one automatically from its frontmatter.
RULESshort
  • Nothing smaller than 22px on the 1200px canvas
  • Six words maximum in the largest text
  • The URL appears on every card — it's the only call to action
  • Case study cards use the tab and the well, so the card looks like the page it opens
  • Dark or lime fill, never paper — the card has to break out of a white feed
Never
  • Put a full case study title in Silkscreen
  • Rely on detail in a screenshot — crop to one legible element
  • Reuse one generic card across every page
  • Place anything important within 40px of an edge
