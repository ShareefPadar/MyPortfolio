There's real production work behind this too. Six years of it sits under NDA, and I'll walk you through it live.
WHAT CHANGED FROM YOURS Same structure — centred heading, one line, email, socials. Three additions: an availability strip at the top, a copy button beside the email, and a "designed & built by" credit. The first two are conversion; the third is the quietest possible proof of your differentiator.
SOCIALS ARE LABELLED, NOT BARE ICONS Your current footer has three unlabelled marks. Behance and Medium in particular aren't universally recognised, and an icon with no label fails on screen readers unless you add hidden text anyway. These use the real brand SVGs at 14px in monochrome — never pixel redraws, since brand marks have usage guidelines.
THE BUBBLE MOVED HERE Your NDA line currently floats over the footer as a magenta bubble. In lime, anchored bottom-left of the dark block, it reads as a deliberate aside rather than a widget. It hides below 820px — on mobile that line belongs in the About page, not overlapping the footer.
THE STATUS STRIP Now location and response time rather than availability. It still does the useful work — a recruiter in London knows you're four hours ahead and that you answer — without announcing anything to your current employer, and it never goes stale. Other options that stay neutral:
