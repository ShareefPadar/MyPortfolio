Aqua, XP and the glossy Web 2.0 that followed: a **fixed-width page on pinstripes**, a **glossy masthead with tabs**, a **sidebar of widgets**, **panels with silver title bars**, **reflections**, **gel capsules**, a **"New!" starburst** and the **yellow info box**.

## Type
Nunito 700/800 (headlines, buttons) · Nunito Sans 400/700/800 (everything else). Verified OFL. Sentence case.

## Gel — the rule carried over from v1
The highlight band is a **fixed 7px**, never a percentage, so it always sits above the label. Measured from rendered pixels at rest and on hover: every gel label passes; pressed only darkens (white on the darkest stop is 7.4:1).

## Reviewed from the first build
- 7 widths (1440 → 320): no horizontal overflow, every target ≥ 44px, all five Nunito weights load
- **Reading order = visual order.** A first version used CSS `order` to pull the photo widget up on phones, so screen readers heard the greeting after the live products while sighted users saw it near the top. Removed: phones stack in DOM order, and the greeting now leads naturally into "Let's connect"
- **Tab focus rings drawn inside the tab.** Outside rings were clipped by the masthead, and the current tab's blue ring vanished against the blue bar. Now blue on white (6.25:1) and lime on blue (5.19:1)
- Thumbnails switch to 16:9 on phones — 4:3 made every item a screen tall

## Phase 2 — home (translates 1995)
`eras/2002/layout.mjs` is the shell every 2002 page uses.
- **Portrait:** the real pixels drawn smooth over a sky gel, in a white frame with a reflection; a gloss sweep on hover. Top of the sidebar on desktop, first on phones.
- **Guide:** an instant-messenger window ("Clippy is typing a message…", a three-line log). Opens when he talks, settles into a buddy pill when he's quiet; a minimise click is respected.
- **Cursor:** an Aqua-style arrow (CSS cursor).
- **Live products:** gel app icons with reflections replace the empty thumbnails.
- **Copy → Copied:** green gel, tick, yellow fade.
- **Era switcher:** a silver gel beside the logo opens an Aqua-style menu (blue gel highlight, ✓ current). Arrival: fade from white.
- **Work:** a portfolio directory — breadcrumb, gel tag pills with "Showing N of 9", a featured panel with reflected thumbnails, a striped list (gel app icon when there's no screenshot).
- **About:** a profile page — portrait in the sidebar, numbered gel beads, a striped CV with gel date pills, education in the yellow info box, a glossy lime pull quote.
- **Case study:** a Web 2.0 article — Aqua barber-pole progress bar, breadcrumb, a details table, the yellow info box for the outcome, white-framed shots, an Aqua window (traffic lights) for the prototype, a gel Next story button.

- **Home game (`game.mjs`):** BUBBLE POP, sitting the way live's Dino Runner does. No box: bubbles hang over the pinstripes from a thin silver rail, across the page's full width, and the launcher is a silver gel cradle sitting on the blue gel footer bar. Loose bubbles fall into the bar. A new row pushes down every 8 shots (dots beside the cradle count them); the dashed line above the cradle is the limit. Score in a silver gel capsule; the title has the logo's reflection.

## Guide (v3)
Clippy, as the Office assistant still looked in 2002 (`content.guide`, live's lines). He reuses live's own pixel sprite (Microsoft's character is not redrawn), stands bottom right with no window, and fidgets with live's idle sequences. His lines come in the pale yellow balloon of the time: Tahoma, black hairline, round corners, a tail down to him, blue bullet options (the other pages and Résumé), a Hide button. Phones: he stands quietly; tap him to talk.
