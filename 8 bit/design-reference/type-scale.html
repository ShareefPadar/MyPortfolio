TYPE SCALE

The face is chosen by what the string is, not by heading level. An H1 can be Silkscreen or Geist depending on whether it's a label or a sentence.

00 — THE GATEask this before every heading
IS THIS READ AS
A SENTENCE?
YES →Geist. Always. Case study titles, section headings, decision titles, captions, body. No exceptions, at any size.
NO →Is it a label or a number?
  LABEL →Departure Mono. Tags, meta, eyebrows, nav links, timestamps.
  STATEMENT →Silkscreen. Hero, page titles, numerals, buttons. Five words maximum.
Why — heading level describes document structure; the face describes reading behaviour. Tying them together is what produces bitmap paragraphs.

06 — RULESshort list
  • Silkscreen never exceeds five words or drops below 1.7 line height
  • Silkscreen Bold for hero, page titles and numerals; Regular for everything else
  • Negative tracking on H1 and H2 only
  • Body capped at 60ch, lead at 52ch
  • Captions are Geist — they're sentences, not labels
  • Mobile drops display sizes hardest; body barely moves
Never
  • Silkscreen for an H1 that happens to be a sentence
  • Mono for anything longer than about six words
  • All-caps Geist — that's what the mono layer is for
  • More than two type sizes inside a single card
