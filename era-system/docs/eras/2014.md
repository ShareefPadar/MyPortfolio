## The idea
The flat-design web of 2013–15 and Material Design's first release: a coloured **app bar**, **bold flat colour blocks** as sections, **image-top cards**, and a **floating action button**. No gradients, no blur, no gloss — colour does the work. **But Material is paper:** the app bar, cards and FAB sit on elevation shadows (z-depth 1–3). iOS 7 flat had no shadows; Material did, and this era follows Material

## Type
Montserrat (300 hero and titles, 600 buttons) · Oswald 400 (labels) · Open Sans 400/600 (reading). All verified OFL; see archive for glyph checks. Statements uppercase via CSS — content is written in normal case.

## Motion
Material's standard curve. Cards **grow** in from 94% once on scroll; the hero headline fades once; the drawer slides 300ms; buttons show a **ripple** from the pointer. Under reduced motion: no ripple, no transitions, nothing hidden.

## Drawer behaviour (tested with a real keyboard)
Closed links are `visibility:hidden`, so Tab skips them · opening moves focus to the first link · Escape, the scrim, a link click or the Close button closes it · focus returns to the burger · `aria-expanded` tracks state · crossing 900px resets it.

## Bugs found by measuring and looking, fixed
- **`<button>` painted the browser's grey face** behind ghost and flat variants — "Copy email" was white on `rgb(239,239,239)`, 1.15:1. Anchors don't do this, so only buttons were wrong. `.btn { background: transparent }`
- **App-bar link hover lightened the blue** behind white text → 4.39:1. The wash now darkens
- **The scrim dimmed the drawer it was opening.** The drawer lives inside the sticky app bar, a stacking context at z-90, so its z-index 120 only competed inside it while the scrim (110) painted over the entire bar. Scrim moved inside the bar. The drawer's own text had measured 3.07:1 because of it

## Second review — what the first pass missed
The first pass only checked contrast at two widths. A full re-check (7 widths down to 320px, touch targets, focus, hover, tablet layout) found:
- **Wrong premise:** the era was documented as "no soft shadows". True of iOS 7, false of Material, whose whole metaphor is paper with elevation. Without shadows the work cards had no edges — their white body sat on a white page. Added z-depth 1 to cards (z-depth 3 on hover), z-depth 2 to the app bar, a soft shadow on the FAB
- **Four targets under 44px:** logo link (22px tall), footer email (30px on mobile), skip link (42px), burger squeezed to 41px wide at 320px. All now ≥ 44
- **Tablet and phone:** the avatar filled a full screen and pushed the work down; it's now ≤ 220px below 900px wide
- **Both live products used the same badge icon** → grid for Roomy, check for Form
- Held up: no horizontal overflow at any width including 320px (400% zoom reflow), focus rings visible on every background, drawer keyboard behaviour, contrast 48/48 · 45/45 · drawer 4/4

## Guide (v3)
A chat head (`content.guides.2014`, Shareef's own voice): his round pixel avatar floating on the right edge, the messaging pattern of 2013–14. It opens a white bubble beside it, shows a red 1 for a message you haven't opened (phones start this way), drags anywhere and snaps back to the nearest side, and dropping it on the × that appears at the bottom sends it away. HIDE in the bubble does the same. The snackbar now only confirms "Copied".
