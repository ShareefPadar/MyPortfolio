Pale blue face, blue outline and label. Stays inside the primary's colour family, so the pair reads as one system rather than two decisions.

Near-white face with a blue outline. Quietest of the four, and the most forgiving next to screenshots.
C — Neutral plate
Grey face, black outline. Reads as a genuine system control rather than a styled link — the most 95 of the three, and the most neutral.
D — Lime
Included so you can rule it out. Lime is lighter and higher-chroma than the blue, so it wins the pair — the secondary ends up louder than the primary.
