SPACING

Ten values, all multiples of the 4px unit. Roughly geometric so adjacent steps are visibly different — a linear ramp gives you values too close to tell apart, which is how spacing drifts.

06 — RULESshort
  • Every value comes from the ramp — 4 8 12 16 24 32 48 64 96 128
  • Space above a heading is 3–4× the space below it
  • Component padding is fixed per component, not adjusted per instance
  • Mobile compresses the large steps only — --s16 and below never change
  • Prose caps at 60ch, lead at 52ch
Never
  • Off-ramp values — no 11px, 15px, 19px
  • Equal space above and below a heading
  • Margin on both sides of a gap — pick one direction and stay with it
  • Padding that varies between two instances of the same component
